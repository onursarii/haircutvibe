Top 15 Trending Winter Hair Colors for 2026

By HaircutVibe Editorial Team · Published September 2, 2026 · Updated September 2, 2026 · 9 min read

From glazed espresso and cherry cola to pearl blonde shimmer, these rich winter shades are designed to flatter your undertone, photograph beautifully and give your next salon appointment a clear direction.

#1 Espresso#2 Honey#3 Copper#4 Mushroom#5 Blackberry#6 Cashmere#7 Cherry Cola#8 Platinum#9 Cinnamon#10 Plum#11 Mocha#12 Rose Gold#13 Chocolate#14 Caramel#15 Pearl

Winter color is all about depth, shine and a shade that works with your real routine. Save your favorites, then show your stylist the placement and finish you want—not just the color name.

1. Glazed Espresso Brunette

A mirror-shine brunette with cool espresso depth and a glassy finish.

Best for: neutral to deep skinOval, square facesMaintenance: Medium

Stylist tip: Ask for a level 4–5 espresso gloss, subtle face framing and a high-shine acidic toner over natural brunette.

2. Frosted Honey Blonde

Soft honey warmth cooled with an icy beige veil for luminous winter blonde.

Best for: fair to medium warm skinHeart, oval facesMaintenance: High

Stylist tip: Request fine balayage with a beige-honey gloss, brighter around the face and a shadow root.

3. Spiced Copper Velvet

Velvety copper-red with cinnamon dimension that glows in winter light.

Best for: warm and olive undertonesRound, diamond facesMaintenance: Medium

Stylist tip: Ask for a copper base with a small amount of red-violet pigment blended through the lengths.

Muted taupe brunette with cool ash dimension and editorial softness.

5. Midnight Blackberry

Near-black brunette lit with subtle violet-blue jewel reflect.

Soft creamy blonde with a plush, expensive-looking neutral finish.

Best for: fair to medium neutral skinOval, heart facesMaintenance: High
