How to style a textured crop

Start with damp hair and use a small amount of lightweight product. Work from the roots and dry the fringe into the direction you want. Fine hair generally benefits from preserving useful weight and avoiding heavy glossy products that separate strands.

For thick hair, internal texture can reduce bulk without destroying the shape. For wavy or curly hair, preserve the natural pattern and use a curl-friendly finish. Show your barber the exact fringe length and perimeter you prefer instead of relying only on the word “crop”.
