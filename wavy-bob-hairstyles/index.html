Wavy Bob Hairstyles 2026

Wavy bobs combine a shorter silhouette with visible texture and movement. The shape can range from a compact chin-length bob to a softer shoulder-grazing cut.

How to choose a wavy bob

Start with your natural wave pattern. A wavy bob can look different depending on how much the hair contracts when dry. Discuss the finished dry length with your stylist rather than relying only on wet-hair length.

Consider layers carefully. Light shaping can encourage movement, while too much layering can change the density and outline. The right balance depends on your hair thickness and natural texture.

Choose a length that fits your routine. Chin and jaw lengths create a more compact silhouette. Longer bobs give extra styling options and can feel easier to manage during grow-out.
