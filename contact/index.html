Contact HaircutVibe

Have a question about an article, found an error, want to report an image-rights concern or have feedback about the website? We welcome credible reports and relevant inquiries.

Editorial corrections

If you notice a factual error, outdated statement or misleading description in one of our guides, please identify the article and explain the issue clearly. We review credible correction requests and update content when appropriate.

Image and copyright concerns

If you believe an image on HaircutVibe is being used without appropriate permission, licensing or attribution, please identify the page and image as precisely as possible and provide the relevant rights information or source details. We take credible rights concerns seriously and review them promptly.

General and business inquiries

For website feedback, editorial questions, partnerships or other business inquiries, please contact the HaircutVibe team through the official communication channel associated with our website or official social profiles.

For privacy-related requests, please also review our Privacy Policy.

What to include

  • The URL of the relevant HaircutVibe page.
  • A short description of your question or concern.
  • For image-rights reports, the image location and source or ownership information where available.
  • For correction requests, the specific statement that needs review and the supporting information.

We may request additional information when necessary to verify a correction, rights claim or other substantive report.
