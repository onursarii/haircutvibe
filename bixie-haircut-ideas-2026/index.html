15 Bixie Haircut Ideas to Save for 2026

Published September 27, 2026 · HaircutVibe Editorial Team · Photo-first guide

The bixie is having a major 2026 moment because it sits between a bob and a pixie. The best versions keep enough length around the face to feel flexible while using shorter layers through the crown and back for movement. Here are 15 visual ideas to save and show your stylist.

Trend context: Vogue describes the 2026 bixie as a bob-pixie hybrid with a casual, undone feel, while Who What Wear highlights its versatility, longer face-framing pieces and textured layers. The photos below are visual references rather than claims that every image is an exact salon bixie. Vogue · Who What Wear

What to save before your appointment

Save two or three photos that show the front, side and back shape you like. Tell your stylist whether you want the result closer to a bob or closer to a pixie, how much length you want around the ears and face, and how much daily styling you are willing to do.

For fine hair, discuss where to preserve weight. For wavy or curly hair, ask how shrinkage and natural movement will change the final length.

FAQ

Is a bixie still trending in 2026?

Yes. Vogue and Who What Wear both feature the bixie prominently in their 2026 haircut coverage, describing it as a hybrid between a bob and a pixie.

How many photos should I show my stylist?

Two or three strong references are usually more useful than a large folder of conflicting shapes. Choose photos that show the length and silhouette you actually want.
