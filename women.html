What is trending in 2026?

Butterfly cuts remain a strong choice for anyone who wants movement and face-framing layers without giving up long-hair versatility. Shorter butterfly bobs bring the same layered idea into a jaw-to-shoulder silhouette. Bob variations are also prominent, from blunt and French-inspired shapes to softer textured versions.

Curtain bangs continue to be one of the easiest ways to change a look without committing to a dramatic overall cut. Bixies and short layered crops are useful for anyone moving toward shorter hair, while wolf cuts and shaggy layers keep the intentionally undone texture that performs well in visual discovery feeds.

For curly and wavy hair, the best reference is the shape rather than the exact photograph. Ask your stylist to work with your natural pattern, preserve enough weight to prevent unwanted shrinkage and decide where face-framing layers should begin. Bring the saved reference and describe the maintenance level you actually want.
