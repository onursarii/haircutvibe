What is trending in 2026?

Wave-led looks are strong because they deliver movement and volume in a visually obvious way. Body waves and loose waves can be adapted to different lengths, while a wavy lob gives the same texture in a shorter silhouette. Butterfly cuts remain attractive for people who want face-framing movement without abandoning long-hair versatility.

Bob variations are another major cluster. Blunt, French-inspired and softer layered bobs give very different results from the same basic length. Curtain bangs are a relatively low-commitment way to change the frame around the face, and bixies, wolf cuts and shag-inspired layers cover the more textured end of the trend spectrum.

For curly and wavy hair, use the photograph as a shape reference rather than expecting an identical result. Ask your stylist to preserve your natural pattern, choose the face-framing point deliberately and explain how much daily styling you are willing to do.
