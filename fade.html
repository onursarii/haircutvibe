How to choose your fade

Low fades keep the transition close to the ear and neckline, making them versatile for professional and casual styles. Mid fades raise the transition and create more contrast. Choose the height according to your face proportions, hair density and how often you want to visit the barber.

For fine hair, preserve useful weight on top and use lightweight matte products. For thick or curly hair, ask for controlled texture rather than excessive thinning. A reference photo works best when you also tell your barber the desired fade height and top length.
