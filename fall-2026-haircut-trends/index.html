20 Fall 2026 Haircut Trends Worth Saving

Published September 6, 2026 · HaircutVibe Editorial Team

We checked the current Southern Living Fall 2026 roundup and selected its first 20 haircut trends. The style names and descriptions below are original HaircutVibe editorial copy.

Checked list: The 20 titles below match the first 20 entries in Southern Living's current article, in the same order. The article itself contains 45 trends in total. cite source omitted in site copy

1. Textured Layers

Fine to medium · Straight to wavy

Layered movement through the mid-lengths and ends adds texture while preserving fullness.

Pin it

2. Short and Wild Shag

Curly to wavy · Medium density

Choppy layers give waves and curls room to expand for an intentionally undone shape.

Pin it

3. Blunt Collarbone Cut

Straight to wavy · Medium to thick

A clean collarbone-length outline gives a lob a strong polished shape.

Pin it

4. Soft Curved Bob

Straight to lightly wavy · Fine to medium

Rounded ends and a softer curve give this bob a refined vintage-inspired shape.

Pin it

5. Wispy Bangs

Straight, wavy or curly

Light airy fringe softens the face and blends naturally into layered lengths.

Pin it

6. Cheekbone-Grazing 90s Bob

Straight · Fine to medium

A crisp cheekbone-length line creates a sleek 1990s-inspired silhouette.

Pin it

7. Mia Wallace Bob

Straight · Graphic bob

A sharp graphic bob with a strong fringe delivers a dramatic retro silhouette.

Pin it

8. Mini Blunt Bob

Straight to wavy · Fine to medium

A chin-grazing blunt perimeter stays strong while subtle texture keeps it modern.

Pin it

9. Blunt Textured Cut

Straight to wavy · Fine to medium

Dense blunt ends combined with subtle texture create a fuller-looking silhouette.

Pin it

10. Curtain Bangs

Straight to curly · Most face shapes

Center-parted fringe frames the face and grows out gracefully into longer layers.

Pin it

11. Short Shag Cut

Wavy to curly · Medium to thick

Chin-length layers and fringe give naturally textured hair a lively shape.

Pin it

12. Sharp Shoulder Cut

Straight · Fine to medium

A precise shoulder-length line makes a classic bob or lob feel modern and intentional.

Pin it

13. 1970s Long Cut

Long · Straight to wavy

Soft feathering and airy volume bring a modern version of the long-haired 1970s shape.

Pin it

14. Lob-Length Curls

Curly · Medium length

Shoulder-skimming curls with light layers preserve bounce without making the ends heavy.

Pin it

15. Choppy Layers

Medium to long · Straight to wavy

Piecey layers add movement and edge while leaving enough length for versatile styling.

Pin it

16. Curly Pixie Cut

Curly · Fine to thick

A cropped shape leaves enough length on top for natural curls to create volume and character.

Pin it

17. Blunt Chin Bob

Straight · Fine to medium

A one-length chin bob creates a dense crisp perimeter and a clean modern silhouette.

Pin it

18. Barely-There Bangs

Fine fringe · Straight to curly

A feather-light fringe adds framing without visually closing off the forehead.

Pin it

19. Textured Fringe Bob

Fine to medium · Straight to wavy

A compact bob with textured layers and fuller fringe gives the classic shape a fashion-forward finish.

Pin it

20. Textured Lob

Medium length · Fine to thick

A mostly one-length lob with controlled texture keeps the shape full while adding an effortless finish.
