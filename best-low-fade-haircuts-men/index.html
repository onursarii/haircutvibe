Styling tips

Bring a reference image, explain your preferred finish, and ask your barber to balance the sides with your natural hair density. Use lightweight matte product for texture and schedule a cleanup every two to three weeks.

Face shape recommendations

Oval and square faces suit most low fade variations. Round faces benefit from controlled height and texture; oblong faces should avoid excessive height.
