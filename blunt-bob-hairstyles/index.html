Blunt Bob Hairstyles 2026

A blunt bob is defined by a strong, clean baseline. These references focus on visible bob shapes and can help you compare length and silhouette before your next haircut.

How to choose a blunt bob

Start with the length. A chin-length bob creates a compact outline, while a neck or collarbone-length version gives more movement and styling flexibility.

Think about the baseline. A very clean line emphasizes the blunt character. A slightly rounded shape can feel softer while keeping the same overall structure.

Match the cut to your texture. Straight hair makes the perimeter especially visible. Wavy hair can soften the edge and add movement. Ask your stylist how your natural texture will affect the final length and shape.
