Editorial Policy

HaircutVibe publishes practical hair inspiration and styling guidance for readers researching a haircut, hairstyle or hair color. Our goal is to make the information clear, useful and transparent about its limitations.

Originality

We aim to provide original explanations, comparisons, styling suggestions and practical context. We do not intentionally publish copied articles or lightly rewritten material from other websites. When outside sources inform a trend article, the article may identify those sources and add original context rather than reproducing their work.

Research and trend coverage

Hair trends can change quickly. For trend-focused guides, we may review current beauty and fashion publications, professional commentary and other relevant public sources. We distinguish current trend reporting from our own practical guidance and avoid presenting a trend as a universal recommendation.

Visual references

Images are used to help readers understand hairstyles and color concepts. We consider source, relevance and usage rights when selecting visual material. Where third-party images or embeds are used, they are not presented as original HaircutVibe photography.

Accuracy and limitations

Hair and beauty outcomes vary with hair type, texture, density, condition, previous treatments and professional assessment. Our guides are informational and do not replace advice from a qualified stylist or barber. We aim to explain practical trade-offs such as maintenance, styling time and the need for regular appointments.

Updates and corrections

Evergreen guides may be reviewed when meaningful information changes. Trend articles may be updated as the relevant season develops. If you identify a factual error, misleading statement or image rights concern, please use the contact method associated with the official HaircutVibe website or its official social profiles. Credible reports are reviewed and material is corrected when appropriate.

Advertising and editorial independence

HaircutVibe may use advertising to support hosting, publishing and maintenance costs. Advertisements are separate from editorial content and do not determine the substance of our guides. Any future commercial relationships will be considered separately from the factual and editorial content of an article.
