Editorial Policy

HaircutVibe publishes practical hair inspiration and styling guidance for readers researching a haircut, hairstyle or hair color.

Originality

We aim to provide original explanations, comparisons, styling suggestions and practical context. We do not intentionally publish copied articles or lightly rewritten material from other websites.

Visual references

Images are used to help readers understand hairstyles and color concepts. Image sources and usage rights are considered when selecting visual material. Where third-party images or embeds are used, they are not presented as original HaircutVibe photography.

Accuracy and updates

Hair and beauty guidance can vary with hair type, condition, previous color treatments and professional assessment. Our guides are informational and do not replace advice from a qualified stylist or barber. We review and update evergreen guides when meaningful information changes.

Corrections

If you identify a factual error, misleading statement or image rights concern, please contact the HaircutVibe team through the contact details associated with the website or its official social profiles. We will review credible reports and correct material when appropriate.

Advertising

HaircutVibe may use advertising to support the site. Advertisements are separate from editorial content and do not determine the substance of our guides.
