Classic Blunt Bob Styling Ideas

A blunt bob is defined by a strong perimeter rather than heavily layered ends. The style can look sleek and graphic or softer and textured depending on length, density and styling.

Choose the length

A chin-length bob creates a strong jaw-level frame, while a slightly longer bob gives more flexibility for ponytails and heat-free styling. Ask your stylist to consider your natural curl or wave before deciding the final dry length.

For straight hair

A clean perimeter and a smooth finish emphasize the architectural shape. A lightweight smoothing product and controlled blow-dry can enhance shine without making fine hair look flat.

For wavy hair

Keep the perimeter intentional but let the natural texture create movement. A light curl cream or mousse can define waves while preserving the blunt outline.

Face shape

Most face shapes can wear a bob when the length and volume are adjusted thoughtfully. A stylist can position the strongest visual line to complement your cheekbones and jaw rather than following a fixed rule for every face.

Maintenance

Blunt lines become less crisp as the hair grows, so regular trims are useful if you want a precise shape. The exact schedule depends on growth speed and how exact you want the perimeter to remain.

What to ask for

Bring a reference photo and describe whether you want a sharp, polished edge or a softer textured finish. Tell your stylist how often you realistically style your hair.
