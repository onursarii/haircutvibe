Curtain Bangs Hairstyles 2026

Curtain bangs frame the face from the center and blend into longer pieces. These references focus on visible fringe shapes and can help you compare how bangs connect with the rest of the haircut.

How to choose curtain bangs

Choose the shortest point carefully. Shorter center pieces create more visible fringe, while longer curtain bangs can blend gradually into cheekbone or jaw-length layers.

Consider your natural texture. Wavy and curly hair can create a softer, less uniform fringe. Straight hair can show the center opening and face-framing transition more clearly.

Think about styling and grow-out. Curtain bangs can be styled away from the face, but the result depends on length and texture. Ask your stylist where the pieces will sit as they grow.
