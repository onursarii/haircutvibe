Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

HaircutVibe is a public website providing haircut, hairstyle and hair color inspiration and guides. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website, why it may be processed, and the choices available to you.

Information processed when you visit

HaircutVibe does not require an account to browse ordinary site content. Depending on the services enabled on the website, hosting, security, analytics and advertising providers may process technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from an IP address, IP address, timestamps and similar network or usage information.

We do not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information in order to browse ordinary site content.

Cookies and similar technologies

HaircutVibe may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, analytics and advertising. These technologies may help remember settings, understand how pages are used, measure traffic and measure or support advertising.

Google advertising and AdSense

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising service is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information about visits and use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure or limit advertising. Depending on the user's choices, applicable requirements and the services enabled, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google may use information collected from visits to this website and other sites or apps that use its services for advertising and measurement purposes. Users can review or manage available personalized advertising choices through Google Ads Settings.

For information about Google's handling of data from sites and apps that use its services, review Google's current privacy information and advertising documentation. Google independently determines some aspects of its processing under its own policies, and HaircutVibe does not control that processing.

Consent and regional requirements

Where consent is required for cookies, personalized advertising or similar processing, HaircutVibe intends to provide the applicable disclosure and consent controls through the services enabled on the website. For users in regions where Google requires a certified consent management solution for relevant advertising services, an appropriate Google-certified consent mechanism may be used.

Visitors can also control cookies through browser settings and any consent controls presented on the website. Some advertising choices may be available through aboutads.info.

Analytics and measurement

HaircutVibe currently uses Google Analytics 4 through Google tag measurement. The Google Analytics measurement ID used by the site is G-WX2LNKCL62. Google Analytics may process information such as page views, traffic sources, device and browser characteristics, approximate location, and interactions with the site. This information is used to understand traffic, measure site performance, identify technical problems and improve content and navigation.

Analytics processing may involve cookies or similar technologies and may vary by region and by the consent choices available to visitors. Google processes data under its own policies and terms. Visitors can review Google's privacy information and use available browser or consent controls to manage applicable choices.

Third-party services and external links

We may rely on third-party services for hosting, security, analytics, advertising, content delivery and embedded functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. HaircutVibe does not control the privacy practices of external websites linked from this site.

Data retention and security

Retention periods depend on the service, the type of information and the purpose of processing. HaircutVibe uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the website and information processed through services it controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of certain personal information. You may also have rights concerning withdrawal of consent. Available rights and procedures can vary by jurisdiction and by the provider responsible for processing.

You can use browser controls and any consent or privacy controls provided on the website to manage available choices. You may also use the relevant controls provided by third-party services, such as Google's advertising settings, where applicable.

Children

HaircutVibe is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the site, please use the contact method associated with the official HaircutVibe website or its official social profiles so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services or applicable legal requirements change. The date above indicates when this version was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact method associated with the official HaircutVibe website or its official social profiles. We will review reasonable privacy requests and respond as appropriate to applicable law and the information available to us.
