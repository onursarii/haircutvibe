Modern French Crop Guide

Texture, fringe and easy daily styling for a contemporary crop.

The French crop is a low-maintenance haircut built around a textured top and controlled fringe. Ask for a gradual taper and a finish suited to your hair density.

Styling and face shape tips

Oval, square and heart-shaped faces work especially well. Use matte clay, dry the fringe forward and avoid overloading fine hair.
