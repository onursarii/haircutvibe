How to choose a pixie haircut

Decide how much length you want to keep. A cropped pixie is easier to keep very short, while a longer or grown-out pixie gives more styling options and can transition toward a bixie.

Consider texture and density. Fine hair can benefit from purposeful texture without removing too much useful weight. Thick or coarse hair may need internal shaping to control bulk.

Think about the grow-out. Ask your stylist where the shape will gain weight first and how often you will need a trim. If you want fewer appointments, a softer perimeter and longer sides can give you more flexibility.
