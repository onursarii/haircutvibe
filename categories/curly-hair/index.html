How to choose a haircut for curls or waves

Consider shrinkage and natural volume. Curly hair can appear shorter when dry, so your preferred final length should be discussed with your stylist on dry hair when appropriate.

Use layers deliberately. Layers can release weight and encourage movement, but too much removal can make some curl patterns expand more than expected. The right amount depends on density and curl pattern.

Protect the natural pattern. Gentle drying, suitable conditioning and lightweight styling products can help preserve definition. A good cut should make your natural texture easier to manage rather than requiring constant heat styling.
