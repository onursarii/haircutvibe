How to choose a men’s haircut

Choose the structure first. Decide whether you want a fade, taper or more natural side transition. The difference affects how sharp the haircut looks and how quickly the sides grow out.

Match the top to your hair type. Straight hair can show precise texture and fringe shapes clearly. Wavy or curly hair often benefits from preserving natural movement instead of forcing the hair into a rigid shape.

Think about maintenance. Skin fades and very short sides usually need more frequent visits to keep the contrast crisp. Tapers, longer crops and textured cuts can offer more room as they grow.
