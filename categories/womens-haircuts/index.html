How to choose a women’s haircut

Start with your natural texture. Straight, wavy and curly hair behave differently when a cut is layered or kept blunt. A useful haircut should work with the amount of movement and volume your hair naturally has.

Then choose the length and maintenance level. Bobs and shorter cuts usually need more frequent shape maintenance, while long layers can preserve length and allow more flexibility between appointments.

Use trends as references, not rules. A current haircut can be adapted by changing fringe length, layering, perimeter shape or styling. Bring a reference image and explain what you like about the shape.
